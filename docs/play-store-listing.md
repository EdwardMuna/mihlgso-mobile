## Full description (max 4000 characters)

MIHLGSO (Mafia Island Higher Learning Graduates and Students Organization) — the official companion app to mihlgso.or.tz.

Stay connected with MIHLGSO wherever you are. Apply for membership, track your contributions, and keep up with the organization's projects and news.

**For members:**
• View your payment and donation history at a glance
• Record new contributions and see your annual subscription status
• Find bank account details for where to pay each contribution type
• See top contributor and donor leaderboards
• Manage your profile and membership details
• Export your payment/donation history as a branded PDF or Excel report, saved straight to your device

**For everyone:**
• Learn about MIHLGSO's mission, leadership, and history
• Browse ongoing and completed projects
• View the photo gallery and latest news
• Apply for membership directly from the app
• Check your application status
• Contact MIHLGSO or reach out via WhatsApp

**For administrators:**
• Manage members, payments, donations, and membership applications
• Approve or reject pending payments and donations
• Export branded reports for any list, matching the website's own document design
• View organization-wide statistics

Available in English and Swahili.
