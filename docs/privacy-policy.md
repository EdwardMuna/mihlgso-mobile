# MIHLGSO Mobile — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 28 September 2026**

This policy explains what information the MIHLGSO mobile app collects, how it is used, and how it is protected. MIHLGSO (Mafia Island Higher Learning Graduates and Students Organization) operates this app as a companion to the MIHLGSO website (mihlgso.or.tz).

## Information we collect

The app connects to MIHLGSO's own servers and does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. The information handled by the app is limited to what members and administrators submit directly:

- **Account information**: name, email address, phone number, and password (stored securely, never in plain text) for members who sign in.
- **Membership details**: education level, graduation year, academic discipline, employer, gender, postal/residential address, and similar fields submitted with a membership application.
- **Profile photo**, if a member chooses to upload one.
- **Payment and donation records**: amounts, contribution type, payment dates, and approval status. The app does not process card or mobile-money payments directly — it records payments that were made through the bank accounts / channels MIHLGSO publishes, for tracking purposes only.
- **Device notifications**: the app requests notification permission solely to alert you when a file you exported (e.g. a PDF/Excel report) has finished downloading to your device.
- **Files you export**: PDF/Excel reports you generate are saved to your device's own Downloads folder. They are not uploaded anywhere by the app.

## How we use this information

- To let members sign in, view their own payment/donation history, and manage their profile.
- To let organization administrators manage membership records, payments, and donations.
- To show organization-wide leaderboards (top contributors/donors) and dashboards, only using data members have already submitted.

## Data sharing

We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties. Data is stored on MIHLGSO's own servers and accessed only by the app and the MIHLGSO website, under the same account system.

## Data retention

Membership, payment, and donation records are retained for as long as your account is active, consistent with MIHLGSO's organizational record-keeping needs. You may contact us to request deletion of your account and associated personal data, subject to any records MIHLGSO is required to keep for financial/organizational accountability.

## Permissions the app requests

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Internet access | To communicate with the MIHLGSO backend. |
| Notifications | To tell you when an exported file has finished downloading. |
| Storage (Android 9 and below only) | To save exported PDF/Excel files to your device's Downloads folder. Modern Android versions (10+) don't require this permission for the same task. |

## Children's privacy

This app is intended for MIHLGSO members, alumni, and stakeholders (adults). It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.
